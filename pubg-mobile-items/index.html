Pick the precise item, not just the nickname

Many items have similar names but different quantities, versions or levels. Look carefully for the exact card, prestige variant, Karambit skin, bundle or star rating in the product title. When multiple listings show the same item at different prices, they may represent separate offers with different fulfillment conditions. Check the listed contents instead of assuming two offers are identical.

How gifting and delivery can work

Some items are described as gifts requiring your PUBG Mobile player ID, a friend request or an in-game interaction threshold. Other items may need an in-game lobby or match. These are listing-specific instructions, not a universal delivery method. Ask whether your game region and mode are supported, whether there is a minimum friendship requirement, and whether delivery is possible during the item event.

Avoid mistakes before paying

Confirm the exact quantity, star level, gift method, any event deadline and the current USD price. A screenshot of an item is not proof that it can be transferred. Never reveal your login password or verification code for a simple gift enquiry. Use the dedicated safety guides to understand game items and account security.

Common questions

What does a six-star item mean?

Some sellers advertise a prestige or collectible variant at six stars. Verify what this means for the specific in-game item and mode before deciding.

Will I need to share my PUBG Mobile password?

For listings fulfilled as a gift by player ID or friend request, a login password should not be necessary. Confirm the actual process before proceeding.

Can I compare multiple prices for the same item?

Yes. Open each offer separately and compare the quantity, variant, transfer method and USD price.
