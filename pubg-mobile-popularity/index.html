Check the popularity amount and card breakdown

PUBG Mobile popularity listings use different units and packaging. For example, an offer might describe a total popularity amount or a pack made up of multiple cards. Check whether 50K means one 50,000-point card, a mixed gift combination or a larger bundle split into individual deliveries. Similar totals may have different conditions.

Popularity, home votes and gifts are different offers

Some products mention home votes or special gifts rather than ordinary popularity cards. These should not be assumed equivalent. Review the exact item description and current event or profile requirements. If you need an amount for a time-sensitive competition, confirm the delivery window and whether that kind of gift counts toward the intended event before making a payment.

Verify your recipient details

Many offers use a PUBG Mobile character ID or nickname to send gifts. Double-check the recipient information and server, and ask for the final USD price, availability, quantity and delivery expectations. Do not share a password or one-time code when only a player ID is required.
