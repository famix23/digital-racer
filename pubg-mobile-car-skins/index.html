Car cards and vehicle skins are not interchangeable

A card, a gift and an already-unlocked vehicle skin can represent different things. Read the offer carefully to identify whether you are purchasing a giftable card, a redeemable event reward or an account that already owns a vehicle skin. Confirm the precise vehicle model and color or variant when stated. A Ferrari-related headline alone does not establish which specific vehicle skin will be received.

Confirm quantity and event availability

Some listings refer to a single card while others mention multi-card bundles or event-specific gifts. Check the number of cards, the in-game event's active status, the recipient region, and any friend or UID requirements. Availability may change quickly when an event ends. Delivery times in supplier descriptions are estimates rather than a guaranteed deadline.

Compare prices safely

Different offers may have materially different price points because of card quantity, variant, or delivery requirements. Review the actual product photos and item description, request confirmation of availability and total cost in USD, and avoid sending a password or login code if the item is supposed to be gifted to a character ID.

Common questions

Is a Ferrari car card the same as owning a Ferrari skin?

Not necessarily. Ask whether the advertised card itself is the delivered item or whether it unlocks a specified vehicle skin under current event rules.

Can I buy more than one car card?

The catalog includes individual and some multi-card offers. The number of cards must be confirmed on the selected product page.

Do all vehicle offers work globally?

No global compatibility should be assumed. Ask about game region, event eligibility and the recipient account before purchasing.
