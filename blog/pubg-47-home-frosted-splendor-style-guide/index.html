PUBG Mobile Frosted Splendor Home Style: Version 4.7 Guide

Published November 15, 2026 · By Digital Racer

PUBG Mobile's Version 4.7 introduces the Frosted Splendor Home style, described in the update as an Ancient Capital-inspired seasonal look. The announced availability begins November 13, 2026 in UTC. Home-mode decorators should distinguish items purchased for appearance from the progression points attached to them, especially when bundles, spins and limited availability overlap.

Find the style and item sources

The announcement lists several possible sources for Frosted Splendor items, including the Home Shop, Lucky Spin, Mysterious Merchant Crate and Craftsman's Pass. That does not mean each item is free or guaranteed from each source. Open the relevant panel and inspect the current inventory, eligibility and cost. Some pieces may be available only through specific promotions or account conditions.

Understand Style Points

The update says purchasing new Home style items can award Style Points, with certain totals enabling reward redemption. That system rewards progression, not necessarily unique interior design quality. Before committing to a purchase, check the actual point value and the threshold of the reward you want. If a piece gives fewer points than expected, avoid additional purchases based on an assumption from an earlier event.

Design rooms for readability

A coherent winter room does not need every available prop. Use one main decorative focal point, group related furniture and leave walking space around interactive objects. Bright ornaments can frame a room, while darker walls help important objects stand out. Test how guests see the entrance and camera angles. These are design principles, not hidden scoring exploits, and they remain useful after the event ends.

Compare acquisition options

Direct store purchases, timed merchants, passes and chance-based spins involve different trade-offs. Read the game-provided price, odds where relevant, and whether an item duplicates something you already own. Track your UC spending against a fixed limit. A rare-looking screenshot should not pressure you into an expensive draw when your preferred design could be built from simpler pieces.

Home Competition and sharing

Version 4.7 also highlights the return of the Home Competition. Read its active submission rules, dates and entry requirements before staging photos or inviting visitors. If you share screenshots, review what personal identifiers appear on the screen. Only claim awards or rankings you can verify from the competition results, and do not use altered images to imply ownership of items that are not actually present.

Keep the project affordable

Write down the exact items you like, where they are offered and which ones are already owned. Build a preview layout before buying more decoration. If the reward system changes, update the list rather than chasing an old threshold. Home customization is most enjoyable when the design stands on its own and the spending remains optional. The live event description always takes priority over promotional posts.
