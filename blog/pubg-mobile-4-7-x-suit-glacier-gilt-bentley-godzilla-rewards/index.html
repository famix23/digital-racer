PUBG Mobile Skins and Events

PUBG Mobile 4.7 Skins: New X-Suit, Glacier M416, Bentley and Godzilla

Version 4.7 is shaping up to be a busy cosmetic season for PUBG Mobile. The publisher has previewed a new X-Suit, Gilt collections, a fresh Glacier M416 animation and timed collaborations. Some events begin before or after the November 12 update itself. This guide separates announced items from unknown draw costs, ownership conditions and speculation.

What new X-Suit was announced?

PUBG Mobile previewed an X-Suit with three upgrade forms, including an advanced golden form. The announcement also paired the suit with an advanced upgradeable M249 cosmetic. Those are promotional descriptions of upcoming cosmetics, not evidence that every player will receive the highest form automatically. Upgrade paths and availability are typically defined in the relevant in-game collection panel.

Players comparing X-Suits should look for the exact name, unlocked stage and effects shown by the game rather than relying on a single screenshot. A promotional video can display a fully developed cosmetic even if the base item initially looks different. Until event rules appear, avoid posting unverified claims about how many UC are required to reach a stage.

The Glacier M416 and weapon-skin previews

One publisher preview mentions a new switch-firearm animation for the Glacier M416. That does not by itself prove that existing Glacier owners will automatically receive every showcased effect without an upgrade or other condition. Check the item interface after the event opens to determine which animation belongs to which form or stage.

Other weapon previews include a Gilt Set featuring an advanced upgradeable S12K and a second Gilt Set associated with an upgradeable GROZA. The first set is also described with a dual-wolf glider, while the second uses frosty visual effects. The important distinction is between what the cosmetic preview shows and what the event actually grants after purchase or redemption.

Royale Pass A22 and other set teasers

The publisher's November 3 preview listed RP A22 among the coming content, alongside a new Hola Buddy and additional sets. Treat this as confirmation that the content was teased, not a complete inventory of Royale Pass rewards. Premium and free tracks, mission conditions and regional scheduling need to be confirmed inside the released event.

Players who collect outfits should review each event's preview panel before spending. Make a note of whether an item is permanent, whether an upgrade is included and whether the advertised effect depends on another level. A vehicle or weapon promotion may feature a package of different items, and the most eye-catching piece is not always the first reward.

Free reward activity begins November 11

A publisher announcement advertises Version 4.7 activity perks beginning November 11, including opportunities to claim pink-tier outfits, weapon skins, furniture and other rewards. The precise qualifying tasks and availability are best checked in the game. A promotional phrase about free rewards does not establish that every player receives every item by logging in once.

Start by opening the Events area and checking the official activity description. Read whether rewards depend on login streaks, missions, exchanges or limited-time redemption. Avoid account-sharing or unauthorized tools promising to bypass the requirements. A small effort to confirm the rules prevents unnecessary spending and disappointment.

Bentley and Godzilla collaborations on November 20

PUBG Mobile announced a Bentley vehicle collaboration for November 20 and a return of Godzilla-themed items on the same date. The teasers identify the collaborations and dates; they do not establish all car models, colors, outfit odds or free unlock methods. The detailed acquisition interfaces may appear closer to release.

For car collectors, note the difference between a collaboration announcement, a specific skin or model and a transferable event card. Do not assume that an item can be gifted to another account simply because a themed vehicle is shown in an advertisement. Likewise, returning Godzilla sets are not necessarily identical to the entire historical inventory of the previous event.

How to follow cosmetics without wasting UC

Keep a watchlist of the items you care about and wait for complete in-game rules. Separate confirmed release dates from rumors, and check whether a collection is region-specific. If an event is chance-based, do not interpret promotional examples as guaranteed outcomes. Track costs through legitimate in-game purchase channels and decide on a spending limit before trying a draw.

The most useful comparison between two cosmetics is often the final appearance, upgrade requirements and personal preference, not an exaggerated rarity label. Our DigitalRacer skin terminology guide explains the differences between upgradeable weapon levels, X-Suit stages and vehicle finishes. New event information can make a future update to this article useful, but rumors will not be treated as facts.
