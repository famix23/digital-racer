PUBG Mobile Seal City Bosses: Metro Royale PvE Enemies

Published November 8, 2026 · By Digital Racer

Seal City was introduced in PUBG Mobile's third Version 4.7 beta as a dedicated PvE environment with several boss and elite enemy designs. These encounters are different from ordinary gunfights because enemies may respond to players in unfamiliar ways. This guide organizes the previewed threats without inventing final health, damage or loot-drop values.

Reaper Scorpion and Aberrant Vine

The beta describes Reaper Scorpion as an escaped laboratory creature and Aberrant Vine as an unnatural growth in underground fissures. Their names and visual roles hint at different encounter shapes, but a descriptive teaser is not a verified attack-pattern chart. Approach unfamiliar bosses from cover, communicate escape routes and observe their animation before committing to an extended stationary attack.

Wasteland Scavenger and unusual monsters

The test also introduces Wasteland Scavenger, whose large pack reflects the scavenging theme. Special monsters include Mimic Mask and Treasure Seal, with descriptions suggesting unusual status or behavior effects. A promotional line about good luck is not proof of a guaranteed reward. Players should test interactions carefully and avoid assuming every creature is a simple damage target.

Heavy elites can pressure entire squads

Codename Earthshaker is portrayed as an enemy with a heavy rocket system, while Barrage carries a multi-barrel gun. Even without final damage statistics, these descriptions suggest threats that could punish groups crowded into a narrow lane. Keep teammates separated enough to avoid one attack affecting everyone and designate clear callouts for when the encounter changes phase.

Ordinary infected still matter

The beta lists Patrol Officer Zombie, Plague Nurse, Aberrant Plant and other threats alongside the bosses. In PvE maps, weaker enemies can become dangerous when they block escape routes or distract players during looting. Save adequate supplies for ordinary encounters rather than spending everything on the most dramatic boss. Be especially cautious around groups that can approach from multiple entrances.

Verify the live roster and rewards

The developer preview explicitly notes that beta content may be altered. Enemy names, attack timing, spawn areas and supplies can change. Do not publish a route that claims guaranteed boss drops unless the public map has been tested under repeatable conditions. Start with scouting and team communication, then record the encounters actually present in the final Seal City mode.

Record encounters without inventing statistics

During a first public Seal City run, note the enemy's last known location, its visible attack cue and which piece of cover gave the squad time to recover. If a boss appears at night in a test preview, confirm whether that condition is required on the released map. Keep track of ammunition and healing used before deciding whether the potential reward was worthwhile. Avoid turning one unusual encounter into a guaranteed spawn or loot-drop chart. A careful log across multiple runs is more useful than a spectacular clip with no context.
