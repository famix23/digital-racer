PUBG Mobile 4.7: Crossing Open Ground in Arctic Chronicles

Published November 18, 2026 · By Digital Racer

Plan staged field crossings using terrain, smoke, vehicles and clear team timing. This Digital Racer guide focuses on decisions a player can check in ordinary gameplay, not secret unlocks or promises of certain results. The exact conditions depend on mode, device and current update, so begin with the rules and settings that the official game actually exposes. Aim for a repeatable process that improves awareness, safety and consistency instead of copying a single dramatic clip.

Survey overlooking terrain

Look for hill crests, windows and rocks that allow opponents to watch the path. A quiet field does not guarantee safety. Prefer a route with short cover-to-cover moves over the shortest straight line.

Split a crossing into stages

Choose two intermediate points before leaving cover. One pair watches while the other pair moves, then trade responsibilities when safe. Avoid stopping in shallow terrain that hides your own view but does not block incoming fire.

Use smoke at the vulnerable moment

Place smoke between an enemy sightline and the squad's movement path. Throwing every smoke early gives opponents time to reposition. Smoke obscures vision; it does not function as physical protection against vehicles or explosives.

Plan a dismount location

Before using a vehicle, agree on where to stop and what hard cover exists nearby. Parking in open ground can leave passengers exposed after a rapid approach. Decorative vehicle skins should never be mistaken for mechanical protection.

Reassess after weather effects

Winter visuals may alter how movement is perceived, but sightlines, audio and ordinary gunfire remain relevant. After an environmental transition, check the route again rather than trusting an old screenshot or a dramatic effect to conceal the squad.

Measure arrival quality

The team should reach the destination together with usable ammunition and healing. Record whether anyone crossed without support or consumed emergency smoke too early. A slightly slower successful rotation is better than four disconnected sprints.

How to test the advice without overreacting

For a useful trial, choose a comparable match scenario and identify the first decision you can control. Keep teammates informed and favor hard cover over a dramatic shortcut. If opponents behave differently, adapt to their actual angles and timing rather than forcing a scripted tactic. Review one avoidable mistake afterwards. The objective is a decision process that works across many lobbies, not a highlight clip or a supposed guaranteed result.
