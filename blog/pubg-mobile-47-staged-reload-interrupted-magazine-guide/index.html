PUBG Mobile Staged Reloading: What Changes in 4.7?

Published November 8, 2026 · By Digital Racer

PUBG Mobile's first Version 4.7 test introduced a staged reload improvement for an empty firearm. Instead of always restarting the entire sequence, the game may preserve the magazine-swap stage when the action is interrupted afterward. The precise timing matters, so this explainer focuses on what the beta actually describes and how to check it after release.

One reload can contain multiple actions

With an empty magazine, a reload involves inserting a replacement and preparing the weapon to fire. The beta describes an interruption after magazine replacement: the next attempt then needs only the remaining chambering action. It does not promise that a player who cancels at any moment will get a loaded weapon. This distinction is important because exaggerated guides might call the mechanic an instant reload trick.

Why fights cause reload interruptions

Players may need to move, use cover, switch weapons or react to an opponent unexpectedly appearing during a reload. A staged system could make a partly completed sequence less punishing, but it does not eliminate the risk of entering a fight without ammunition. A secondary weapon can remain a better option than trying to finish a reload while an opponent is already attacking.

How to practice safely

Use a repeatable practice environment after the final update, empty a familiar gun and observe the reload animation. Test an early interruption and a later interruption separately. Note when magazine insertion visually completes and whether the resumed action differs. Avoid using ranked matches as the first place to experiment with a newly changed mechanic. Do not use scripts or file modifications to force animation states.

Limitations you should expect

The public patch may restrict staged reloading to certain firearms or adjust the animation timing. The beta announcement does not provide a complete weapon-by-weapon list. An interrupted reload could also behave differently depending on remaining ammunition or other game states. Avoid claiming universal applicability until several ordinary weapons have been tested on the release build.

Practical fight discipline remains the same

Reload behind cover, stay aware of footsteps and communicate when a teammate needs protection. Finish preparation before crossing an exposed doorway when possible. If an enemy pushes, prioritize survival rather than exploiting a staged animation you have not mastered. PUBG Mobile's control and performance settings can affect how smoothly the animation appears without changing the actual underlying reload mechanic.

A controlled reload comparison

Take a familiar firearm into an ordinary practice area, empty the magazine and begin reloading. Interrupt once before the magazine is visibly replaced and again after that stage, then observe what must be completed before firing. Keep the same weapon and conditions across attempts. If the outcome differs, record which part of the animation was retained. If it does not, first verify that your released game version supports the specific staged mechanic. This is a learning exercise, not an invitation to use macros or exploit animation cancellation.
