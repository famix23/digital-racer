PUBG Mobile 4.7 Fishing Guide: Where It Fits Into a Match

Published November 15, 2026 · By Digital Racer

Fishing is one of the less combat-focused additions highlighted in PUBG Mobile's Version 4.7 notes. The update describes fishing spots along rivers and coasts, with different fish appearing in different waters. Players should see it as optional in-match activity whose usefulness depends on mode rules and nearby danger, rather than a guaranteed route to premium loot or competitive advantage.

Check whether the mode supports fishing

Official update details list fishing for themed and Classic Erangel, certain Brainrot experiences, PvE maps and Erangel Subzero. That does not establish identical rewards or availability in every playlist. Open the active mode description and find a usable fishing location through the game's own interface. If a video shows fishing in a private creator map, do not assume the same controls work in a ranked Erangel lobby.

Water choice and map awareness

Because fish species may vary by water type, rivers and coastlines may offer different opportunities. The notes do not guarantee that every shoreline produces equal results. Choose a spot near cover with a safe route back to normal looting. Before interacting, check zone position and remaining match time. An exposed shore can be a bad place to stand still if opponents are rotating toward nearby bridges.

Choose the right moment

Early-game fishing may compete with essential armor, ammunition and weapon collection. In midgame, a quiet area behind the rotation might offer a low-pressure chance to explore the mechanic. In the final circles, survival usually matters more than optional activities. Ask your squad before stopping so teammates do not advance without support. Avoid presenting fishing as a universally optimal XP, currency or loot farm.

Understand the reward claim

Separate what the game explicitly awards from what creators speculate about. A cosmetic-looking fish, event point or temporary inventory icon may not be sellable or transferable. Read the item description and final results screen before making claims about value. If an achievement requires a particular species, verify the exact mode and body of water rather than relying on unsupported coordinates shared in comments.

Make a repeatable learning test

Try the mechanic in several short sessions with a consistent approach: note map, mode, water type, interaction and any stated reward. Repeat only with visible, in-game tools; no macro or modified client is needed. Compare outcomes across locations, but remember that small samples can vary because of randomness. Record what you personally observed separately from what official patch notes confirmed.

When not to fish

Skip the activity if your team has a downed member, the zone is closing, the shoreline is being watched or your inventory is missing essential ammunition. Players looking for fast rank progression should evaluate whether the activity fits their actual goal. Fishing can add variety to the winter update, but the safest and most enjoyable use is an optional detour, not a forced task on every match.
