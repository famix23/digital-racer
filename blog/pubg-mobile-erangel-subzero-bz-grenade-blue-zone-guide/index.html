PUBG Mobile BZ Grenade: Blue Zone Tactics in Subzero

Published November 8, 2026 · By Digital Racer

The 4.7 beta describes a BZ Grenade that creates a temporary small blue-zone effect in Erangel Subzero. Because its damage can stack with the map's ordinary zone, it could change how players move through cover. It is also an easy mechanic to misunderstand, and the final damage numbers have not been established by the test descriptions reviewed here.

The basic mechanic

After the pin is pulled and the countdown finishes, the device creates a blue zone that expands, lasts for a period and then shrinks away. The beta specifically notes that its effect can stack with the main map zone. That means the grenade is not merely a colored smoke cloud. Players should be cautious about standing within its area, even when they are otherwise protected by terrain.

Use it for area pressure, not assumptions

A temporary hazard might encourage an opponent to abandon a doorway, rooftop corner or narrow passage. But a grenade does not guarantee an elimination. The target may have an alternate route, recovery resources or special equipment. Think about what position you are trying to deny and whether teammates can cover the exit. Throwing it without a shared plan may simply waste a limited item.

Respect the main circle

When a play zone is already shrinking, a localized second hazard can make the normal route more dangerous. Check where safe terrain is before committing to a flank. A player escaping one blue-zone field may run directly into another. Do not assume the device's effect is harmless to you or your squad; test the live rules in a low-pressure environment first.

Interactions with other equipment

The beta also described a Thermal Protection Suit that blocks blue-zone damage while durability lasts. That creates an interesting possible interaction, but exact stacking behavior, consumption rates and protections should be tested after release. It would be misleading to promise a guaranteed counter based solely on a sentence from the beta announcement. Keep an ordinary route out of danger.

Verify the released version

Erangel Subzero was presented as a special later-mode addition and its beta systems may be adjusted. Observe the final grenade fuse, area size and duration rather than copying unofficial numbers. If you cannot find the BZ Grenade in Classic Mode, that may be because it is mode-specific, not because a hidden file needs unlocking. Use supported channels and ordinary training opportunities.

Use the hazard with a follow-up plan

If an opponent is holding a doorway, a BZ Grenade might push them away from that position. Before throwing, coordinate which exit teammates will watch and where your own squad can move if the temporary zone blocks an approach. Pay attention to the main zone timer because the beta describes stacking effects. When the grenade dissipates, the original sightline may become dangerous again. A well-used hazard supports movement and information; it should not be advertised as guaranteed damage or an automatic win.
