PUBG Mobile 4.7 Ancient Beast: Seal, Medal and Aurora Realm

Published November 8, 2026 · By Digital Racer

The Version 4.7 third beta test describes a dramatic Ancient Beast event with interactive body parts spread across Erangel, a sealing sequence at the creature's head and a separate Aurora Realm. These are unusual themed-mode mechanics, but their exact locations and final rewards may change before release. This preview explains the sequence without claiming an unverified shortcut or guaranteed loot.

The Sealing Ground at the creature's head

The October 16 beta notice describes players attacking the Ancient Beast's head to expose an entangled Glyph Tower. An altar within the tower then releases chains that seal the creature and force its mouth open. This is presented as an event interaction, not simply an ordinary combat boss with a health bar.

The same sequence was described as awarding a Hero's Medal after activating the altar. Before trying it in a live match, check whether the game gives clear interaction prompts and whether other squads can contest the location. A showcase video does not establish that players can complete the objective safely every time.

Entering the Ancient Beast

According to the beta, players can enter a shared space inside the creature once the sealing process has opened its mouth. Attacks against other players are disabled within that inner area in the preview. That creates an unusual interval of exploration or reward collection separate from normal shooting.

The test notes say a player with the Hero's Medal can receive an Aurora Pegasus at a pedestal, while other visitors may get ordinary rewards. This does not imply everyone entering obtains the mount or that it remains in the permanent inventory. Confirm the actual medal requirements after the event launches.

The tail and underwater tower

Another described interaction involves the Ancient Beast's tail wrapped around a submerged tower. Sounding a horn activates the tail and raises the tower from the water, allowing exploration. This is an environmental puzzle-style sequence whose timing could make teams cluster around an obvious landmark.

Players should observe the surrounding terrain before using any event interaction. A newly opened tower may tempt several groups to approach together, and a temporary objective can distract from the normal play zone. Even when an event room is peaceful, the route leading to it may not be.

Caves, shells and moving body platforms

The beta describes body sections blocking access to caves, which can be moved away by shooting them. Other shell sections reveal scales and drop supplies when specific areas are hit. These are environmental targets, distinct from directly damaging an opponent, and might reward players who learn to recognize the visual cues.

Other body parts form shifting platforms on the ground or water. A moving surface can create a temporary crossing but also leave players exposed if their timing is poor. Try predictable low-risk routes before relying on those platforms during a late-zone rotation.

Aurora Realm and a possible return chance

The announcement also describes eliminated players entering Aurora Realm through spectating during its event period. The test involved chasing starlight and completing a relaxing interaction for a chance to return to the battleground. This is a conditional beta mechanic, not an unconditional extra life in every match.

Read the live event instructions carefully if this feature appears. A return mechanic might depend on timing, mode and what other players are doing. Avoid telling squadmates that elimination has no consequences, because final return rules may differ from the test and may not be available throughout a match.

How to approach the event without false promises

Keep ordinary battle-royale priorities in mind: safe landing, supplies, zone movement and clear teammate communication. A complicated objective is easier to complete when the team understands who is covering routes and who is interacting with event objects. Do not enter an exposed area solely because a promotional screenshot shows a rare reward.

All Ancient Beast details here are based on a beta-stage announcement. The game publisher says these features can change. DigitalRacer will not publish alleged guaranteed medals, hidden rewards or exploit paths without checking the public version. Use the in-game event interface to confirm the final sequence.
