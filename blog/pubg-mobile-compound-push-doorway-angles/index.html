PUBG Mobile Compound Push Guide: Doorways, Windows and Trade Angles

Published November 18, 2026 · By Digital Racer

Coordinate building entries without feeding single-file fights to defenders. This Digital Racer guide focuses on decisions a player can check in ordinary gameplay or through official settings, not secret unlocks or promises of certain results. Exact conditions depend on mode, device and current update, so begin with the rules and controls the game actually exposes. Aim for a repeatable process that improves awareness, safety and consistency instead of copying a single dramatic clip.

Count possible defender positions

Before entering, identify open windows, stairways and side doors. A building may have more than one line of fire across the entrance. Observe from hard cover instead of assuming the nearest doorway is unguarded.

Assign entry and trade players

The first player clears the initial angle while a second player is close enough to challenge a defender who fires. Remaining teammates watch outside flanks. Entry does not mean rushing blindly; the team should know what happens after the first contact.

Use utility to solve a problem

Smoke can obstruct a window, while other permitted utility can force a defender to move. Throwing everything at one door may leave no option for the upstairs fight. Check whether an opponent can simply reposition around the effect.

Avoid stacking at one threshold

Several players standing in a doorway can block one another and create easy targets. Use supported cover positions and stagger movement. Communicate clearly if the first entry is abandoned so teammates do not continue into a closed angle.

Reset after an unsuccessful entry

If a teammate is knocked and the route is exposed, protect recovery rather than sending everyone through the same lane. Consider an alternate door or a temporary retreat. Not every compound must be taken before the next zone.

Reassess the building after control

Once inside, check windows and outside approaches rather than celebrating too early. A third party can attack while the squad loots. Prepare the next rotation and share healing before the circle forces another risky movement.

Apply this method and compare results

For a useful trial, choose a comparable match scenario and identify the first decision you can control. Keep teammates informed and favor hard cover over a dramatic shortcut. If opponents behave differently, adapt to their actual angles and timing rather than forcing a scripted tactic. Review one avoidable mistake afterwards. The objective is a decision process that works across many lobbies, not a highlight clip or a supposed guaranteed result.
