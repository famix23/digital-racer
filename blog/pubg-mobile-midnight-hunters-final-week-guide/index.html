PUBG Mobile Midnight Hunters: Final Week Tasks and Rewards

Midnight Hunters introduced a hunter-versus-vampire theme during PUBG Mobile Version 4.6. Its official event period was listed through November 10 at 20:59 UTC, so players near the deadline should focus on feasible objectives rather than trying to complete every activity shown in old promotional footage. This guide explains a practical final-week review.

Identify the actual deadline

The Version 4.6 update announcement gives a scheduled end time for the themed mode, not a promise that every promotional store offer ends identically. Open your Events interface and compare each mission's timer with the general game announcement. Some rewards may be collected separately from the moment a match ends. If the interface shows regional timing or a changed deadline, use the current in-game description as the practical guide.

Choose activities that fit your time

Look at incomplete missions and estimate what they require: ordinary matches, repeated participation, particular modes or a specific game action. A few remaining sessions are best spent on goals you can realistically finish. Do not spend UC impulsively to recover progress unless the rules clearly explain the benefit. An attractive high-tier image is not proof that the item can be earned with one final match.

Keep themed modes separate

Midnight Hunters is the headline vampire-hunter experience, but Version 4.6 also featured Metro Royale and other independent activities. A mission in one system may not count toward another. Open each mode's dedicated screen instead of assuming all seasonal rewards share one progress bar. Recognize whether a screenshot belongs to ordinary Classic, a temporary event, Royale Pass or a completely different spin-off mode.

Avoid end-of-event scams

Urgent messages promising free outfits or exclusive unlocks on the final day should be treated carefully. A seller's chat screenshot or a link asking for a one-time login code is not an official reward method. Do not install modified clients that claim to keep expired events running. Keep linked login services secure and avoid giving remote access to your device to claim a normal game reward.

Learn from the outgoing mode

The closing week is also a chance to identify what worked for you in squad fights, movement and communication. Note your comfortable camera settings and how you handle unfamiliar themed objectives. Those habits carry into new seasons even when the special abilities disappear. Version 4.7's winter preview is a different set of content, so wait for the official release before assuming that a Halloween mechanic will remain active.

Build a final-day claim list

Write down each remaining mission, its reward and the approximate number of ordinary games needed. Mark items that are already claimable first. If a task requires a particular mode, check that it remains selectable before planning a long evening. Consider whether you have enough time for the required repetitions and ignore tasks that cannot reasonably be finished. After claiming a reward, verify that it appears in your inventory instead of trusting an animation. No event countdown justifies giving a stranger your verification code or installing a tool said to extend an expired mode.
