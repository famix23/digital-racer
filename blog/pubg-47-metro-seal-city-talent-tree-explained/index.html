PUBG Mobile Metro Royale Seal City: Combat, Exploration and Survival Talents

Published November 15, 2026 · By Digital Racer

PUBG Mobile 4.7 introduces a reworked Metro Royale Talent Tree with three paths: Combat, Exploration and Survival. The update also launches Seal City PvE content, where teams fight enemies, gather supplies and extract. A new player does not need to memorize every upgrade immediately. Understanding the purpose of each path is more useful than copying an unverified maximum-damage build from a short clip.

Know the three announced paths

Combat, Exploration and Survival are the names used in the official update announcement. The description says many previous talents remain while additional abilities are available. It does not make one tree universally superior, because rewards and survival chances depend on your approach. Open the live Talent Tree and read the actual cost and effect of an upgrade before spending a point.

When Combat investment makes sense

Players who repeatedly fail at enemy encounters may benefit from inspecting combat-related improvements. That does not guarantee that any one talent is the best investment or that it overcomes poor positioning. Learn how the current PvE enemies attack, carry appropriate supplies and coordinate targets. A small upgrade combined with a reliable weapon and careful use of cover can be more valuable than blindly maximizing damage.

When Exploration is useful

Seal City includes searchable containers and collectible story material, according to the official notes. A player who wants to learn the map or complete the Gallery system might prioritize exploration-oriented tools when the live tree offers them. Read what the actual skill changes before equipping it. Exploration also means knowing a safe path between objectives and leaving sufficient time to extract rather than searching indefinitely.

When Survival protects a run

Survival-related upgrades may appeal to players who lose valuable loot because they are caught between encounters. Do not assume any specific defensive percentage without reading the live talent text. Prioritize knowing where you can recover, how much healing you carry and when to stop fighting. A successful extraction often protects more long-term value than one additional optional chest opened with no safe exit plan.

Spend Talent Points deliberately

The announcement says the Fame level cap increases and that additional Talent Points can be earned. This makes allocation a longer-term decision, not an excuse to purchase or exploit points. Record the requirements of a target talent and check whether upgrades can be reassigned. Never use a modified client or memory editor claiming to grant unlimited progression; the risk is not justified by a short-term advantage.

Build around your squad's actual roles

Discuss whether the next run is about combat experience, item collection or safe extraction. Assign jobs accordingly rather than requiring every member to choose identical talents. Review the results of several runs, including what forced an early retreat. Only then change your build. An effective talent plan is one you understand and can adjust as Seal City's enemies, rewards and patches evolve.
