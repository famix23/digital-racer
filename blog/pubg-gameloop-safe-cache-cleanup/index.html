Identify where failure occurs

Note whether the issue appears at GameLoop launch, game asset loading or match entry. Temporary cache cleanup is not a universal fix for CPU throttling or network disconnects. Do not delete folders merely because they appear large.

Exit the emulator and verify its processes have ended before moving temporary files. Active programs can recreate or lock data. Prefer the program's official repair or cleanup controls where available because directory layouts can change.

Distinguish temporary from persistent

Keep game downloads, screenshots, account recovery material and unrelated applications intact. Avoid broad registry wipes and one-click system cleaners that do not show exact targets. If a folder's purpose is uncertain, do not touch it.

Keep account recovery available

Before reinstalling, confirm access to the approved login method. Never paste authentication codes, social cookies or session tokens into a cleanup utility. A cleaner cannot promise to restore deleted account material after the fact.

Test after a single change

Escalate safely

How to test the advice without overreacting

For a reliable comparison, note the present settings and record the symptom before changing anything.
