PUBG Mobile Metro Royale Chapter 34 to Seal City: What Changes?

Published November 8, 2026 · By Digital Racer

Metro Royale is not the same experience as ordinary PUBG Mobile Classic Mode. Version 4.6's chapter schedule reaches an end in November, while the Version 4.7 third beta previews a new PvE map named Seal City. Understanding the difference prevents players from misreading an upcoming feature as if it were already part of Chapter 34.

The chapter's published schedule

The official 4.6 update announcement scheduled its Metro Royale season through November 10 at 20:59 UTC. Check outstanding tasks and in-game reward rules before the listed deadline. Inventory, currency and progression rollover rules can differ between chapters, and screenshots from older Metro seasons are unreliable evidence of what carries over. Use the latest game menu rather than assuming a reset or preservation rule without confirmation.

Seal City is a different PvE setting

The third 4.7 beta describes a large infected coastal town with autumn scenery, a day-night cycle, bosses and enemy dormancy. That differs from a conventional squad fight on Erangel. Players will need to observe enemy behavior and extraction conditions rather than applying the exact same hot-drop strategy. The preview does not establish a complete final map or every possible enemy spawn.

New preparation tools were tested

Equipment Vouchers, consumable presets and a more convenient ammunition purchase interface were described in beta material. These could reduce repetitive loadout work, but they do not eliminate inventory decisions. Learn how much each run costs and whether your expected reward justifies that loadout. It is unwise to invest heavily in items because someone claims a particular beta enemy drops guaranteed high-value loot.

Loot containers may expose you longer

The test changed searching so containers reveal items progressively instead of displaying everything at once. That creates a potential tradeoff between time spent looting and risk. In squad play, one person can watch an approach while another interacts with the container. The backpack's two-column layout and Lock Box management were also previewed, making organization important for efficient extraction.

Start the new phase cautiously

When Seal City becomes available, begin with a conservative route and verify enemy behavior, safe regroup points and the live extraction rules. Do not assume beta boss names and rewards survived unchanged. Keep a fallback plan if nightfall changes enemy activity. Treat official update notes and the live mode selector as authoritative before declaring a route or loadout permanently optimal.

Plan one low-risk scouting run

When Seal City becomes playable, start with an affordable equipment set and treat the first session as reconnaissance rather than a maximum-value loot expedition. Identify entry routes, safe cover, types of infected enemies and a reliable path out. Note whether time of day affects the danger along the route. If the team must withdraw, ask what changed rather than blaming a lack of rare equipment. Repeat the successful parts before visiting higher-pressure areas. An observation-based route is more reliable than a list of guaranteed boss rewards copied from beta footage.
