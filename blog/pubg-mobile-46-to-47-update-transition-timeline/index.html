PUBG Mobile 4.6 to 4.7: November Transition Timeline

Published November 8, 2026 · By Digital Racer

PUBG Mobile Version 4.6 and the upcoming 4.7 winter release have overlapping announcements but different schedules. Players who assume one patch date controls everything can miss claimable event rewards or expect a beta mode too early. This timeline guide separates the systems and highlights the practical checks worth making.

What ends with Version 4.6

The publisher scheduled Midnight Hunters from September 8 to November 10 at 20:59 UTC, and its Metro Royale chapter occupied a similar period. Season 32 Classic and Casual activities were listed through November 12. These dates appear in the Version 4.6 announcement, but the live event menus remain important if a deadline is changed. Different activities should not be treated as one universal switch that removes every seasonal feature at the same moment.

What the new winter patch introduces

Version 4.7 beta announcements describe a winter environment, Glyph abilities, the Aurora Pegasus and the Ancient Beast. These changes were tested in themed modes and may not be available in regular Classic matches. Players should wait for release notes and use the mode selector to identify what is actually playable. A cinematic teaser or beta clip is not evidence that a feature works on every map.

Classic changes may be more immediate

The early beta highlighted consumables, staged reloading and vehicle camera improvements, followed by a new Canted Foregrip. These are areas to test regardless of whether you care about themed events. After installing an official update, practice one familiar weapon and a few healing actions before committing to ranked matches. Do not overhaul your control layout based on unverified beta values.

Do not move December content into November

Erangel Subzero was described as a later winter spin-off with blizzards, frozen doors, satellites and special equipment. A developer preview may show playable footage while the mode remains unavailable to the broader public. Check each feature's own activation schedule. Announcing that a December mode is already live on November 12 would mislead players and potentially push them toward unsafe unofficial installers.

Five-minute transition routine

Review remaining event claims, record important account settings, ensure linked logins are secure and check free device storage. Use supported update channels for mobile or GameLoop. Once the release arrives, read the mode selector first and verify where the new content belongs. Keeping these checks separate prevents a routine game update from turning into a risky search for fake unlock downloads.

Example of overlapping clocks

Suppose Midnight Hunters has closed but the ranked-season screen still shows time remaining, while promotional posts advertise a December snow mode. Those observations can all be true. One concerns a temporary event, another competitive rank and the last future content. Write each deadline beside its feature and convert the published time zone carefully. Doing so prevents an inaccurate headline saying that the entire next update has already arrived. Players can still prepare storage and review settings without assuming that every teased reward becomes available at one global instant.
