Inspect the landing area

Before moving rapidly, identify roofs and ridges overlooking the destination and where you could take cover. A spectacular arrival to an open platform can be worse than a slower route. Know the exit before committing.

Keep the squad within support range

Do not clear an entire building while teammates remain too far away to cover a knock. Agree on a rally point and a maximum separation. If another player cannot use the same movement option, select a route they can actually follow.

Expect danger after the move

The vulnerable moment may be the landing rather than the journey. Reorient the camera, listen and check the nearest doorway before turning the move into a push. Give teammates a short location call instead of silently exploring.

Use mobility defensively

Escape options can be more valuable when a vehicle route is cut off or a fight develops from two directions. Decide the retreat direction in advance so the squad does not have to improvise while under concentrated fire.
