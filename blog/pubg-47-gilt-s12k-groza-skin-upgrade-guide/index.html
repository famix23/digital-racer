PUBG Mobile 4.7 Gilt S12K and Groza: Upgrade Skin Guide

Published November 15, 2026 · By Digital Racer

The Version 4.7 cosmetic announcements identify two new Gilt Set themes, including a Great Wolf Fenrir set accompanied by an upgradable S12K firearm finish and an ice-themed set associated with an upgradable Groza finish. Players comparing these items should focus on current ownership, upgrade requirements and cosmetic value rather than assuming every advertised animation comes with the base unlock.

Know which weapon each finish belongs to

S12K and Groza serve different combat roles, even when both receive elaborate cosmetic treatments. Confirm the exact weapon name in the event panel because themed art can put several items together on one banner. A decorative set does not automatically grant a matching fully upgraded gun. Check whether the offer gives a base firearm finish, a set piece, an upgrade material or a randomized chance at any of them.

Separate unlock and upgrade stages

An upgradable finish may have multiple visual milestones, but the number and effects should be read from the live upgrade interface. The first unlock is not proof that advanced animations, elimination messages or special crates are included. If an online listing says maxed, ask for evidence of the current in-game stage. Upgrade material requirements can vary, so copying costs from a completely different weapon is misleading.

Assess S12K's practical fit

The S12K is a shotgun, so its normal gameplay role differs from a rifle used across medium distances. A cosmetic finish does not change the underlying need to aim, manage magazine capacity and choose engagement range. If you rarely use shotguns, a premium S12K skin may deliver less personal enjoyment than a more frequently equipped item. Style preference is a valid reason to collect, but it is separate from competitiveness.

Assess Groza's practical fit

The Groza is more commonly treated as a powerful close-to-midrange assault rifle, subject to normal game balance and supply rules. A spectacular finish cannot guarantee access to the weapon in every match. Before paying for an upgrade, consider whether the visual effects remain appealing during the actual gameplay camera angle and whether the gun fits your preferred mode. Avoid treating a cosmetic as an aim or damage upgrade.

Avoid assumptions about event pricing

A promotional image may combine different rewards with different acquisition routes. Record each guaranteed item, chance-based item and required upgrade resource separately. Review official probability and spending information where the event provides it. The most responsible comparison is total cost to reach the exact appearance you want, with no assumption that every draw will produce the rare item quickly.

Document the item accurately

For a collection comparison, note the set name, weapon type, current level, upgrade stage and confirmed effects. An image of a Gilt banner shows the event's art, not necessarily a specific account's inventory. Keep purchase claims, transfer rules and item ownership separate. PUBG Mobile's publisher policies govern accounts and items; an unofficial skin unlocker or account-modifying tool does not confer legitimate ownership.
