GTA VI: 34-Track Soundtrack Album vs Justice Score

Published November 8, 2026 · By Digital Racer

Grand Theft Auto VI has two major music releases connected to its launch. Rockstar announced a 34-track official soundtrack album for November 19, 2026 and a separate 30-track original score album composed by Justice for December 11. The distinction matters because songs used in marketing, in-game radio and adaptive score are not all the same product.

The official soundtrack album

Rockstar's September 17 announcement describes GTA VI: The Album as a 34-track project created with Atlantic Records and scheduled alongside the game's November 19 launch. The release includes original tracks by artists from multiple genres. The announced album can be pre-saved digitally and also has physical formats. Do not confuse the album's track count with the number of songs available on all in-game radio stations.

The original score is separate

Rockstar later announced an adaptive original score by French electronic duo Justice. The score responds to how players experience scenes and the world during Jason and Lucia's story. Its separate 30-track album is scheduled for December 11. A score cue that changes during a mission is not equivalent to choosing a radio song from a station playlist, even if both contribute to the game's atmosphere.

The launch trailer uses new music

Rockstar's October launch trailer featured the Justice single Flower in a Hurricane, with named collaborators including Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. A trailer's use of a song does not tell us where that composition appears during gameplay. Do not invent a mission title or story ending based on music placement in edited promotional footage.

Radio adds another layer

The publisher has separately shown stations covering Latin music, country, metal, pop and other genres, alongside on-demand podcasts. These in-game channels are an interactive listening experience rather than simply an album. The released game may include far more station material than the tracks on one physical record. A soundtrack purchase also should not be mistaken for a license to redistribute the music in your own videos.

How to follow each release

Keep three dates or contexts separate: November 19 for the console game and the 34-track soundtrack album, December 11 for the original-score album, and the actual in-game radio interface revealed at launch. Check official product details if you are buying a physical format. Don't trust purported leaked tracks or copied playlists as complete, and remember that music rights can restrict reuse.

Choose the release for your listening style

A listener interested in artist singles and a varied set of genres may prefer the 34-track soundtrack album released alongside the game. Someone who wants the dramatic compositions that respond to Jason and Lucia's story may be more interested in Justice's later original-score album. A physical collector should compare formats, shipping and included editions separately. Neither album purchase automatically gives permission to reuse full songs in monetized videos. Keep in-game radio, soundtrack releases and adaptive score as distinct products when describing the music.
