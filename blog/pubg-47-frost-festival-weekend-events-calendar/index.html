PUBG Mobile 4.7 Frost Festival: Weekend Events and Reward Planning

Published November 15, 2026 · By Digital Racer

PUBG Mobile 4.7 combines the Arctic Chronicles season with Frost Festival experiences that do not all begin or end on the same day. An effective event plan separates permanent account progress from temporary activities and makes space for school, work and normal play. This guide explains what to check in the event panel without inventing rewards or promising free premium cosmetics.

Know the broader season window

The announced Arctic Chronicles themed mode began on November 11, 2026 and is scheduled through January 12, 2027 in UTC. Other activities may use their own schedules, so the season date is not proof that every draw, creator map or reward remains available until January. Always open the individual event details and read the countdown inside the game before spending time or currency.

World of Wonder Frost Festival

The official 4.7 notes include Frost Festival Brainrot gameplay and a seasonal environment in World of Wonder. Laser and Bell series Brainrots were announced, with more additions planned during the version. Look for the creator or event description to identify what counts toward a reward. An entertaining community-made map may not automatically progress an official task, and unofficial advertisements can exaggerate its prizes.

Plan around weekend activities

The update describes a limited-time administrator event on weekends and a Crazy Chicken Day rule covering Friday through Sunday. Those are distinct activities that can require different game modes or entry points. Check the relevant panel at the start of your local weekend, record any reset times and complete the limited task before moving to open-ended matches. Do not assume the event follows local midnight everywhere.

What to prioritize first

Start with rewards that have an explicit expiry and need only a short task, such as logging in or completing a limited mission. Next, tackle objectives that overlap with the modes you already play. Save long progression goals for sessions when your squad is available. This approach reduces the temptation to spend UC simply because a banner displays a countdown, and it leaves room to enjoy normal ranked games.

Distinguish free rewards from draws

A free event item, discounted draw and paid cosmetic bundle are different offers. Open the item description and note whether it is temporary, claimable or subject to randomized outcomes. An advertised trial card does not mean permanent ownership. Keep receipts only for actual purchases, review region-specific availability and avoid services that claim to grant all limited skins without participating in supported in-game events.

A two-minute daily check

Open announcements, review your most time-sensitive mission, then confirm whether today's mode actually offers progress. If nothing expires soon, stop checking and play normally. At the end of the week, revisit any unclaimed rewards and take screenshots of completed tasks for your own records. Digital Racer's editorial calendar will describe major confirmed updates, but the live in-game event panel remains the authority for eligibility.
