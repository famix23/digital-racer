PUBG Mobile 4.7 Update: Release Date, Arctic Chronicles and What's New

Published November 8, 2026 · By Digital Racer

As of November 8, 2026, PUBG Mobile Version 4.7 is scheduled for November 12. The publisher is presenting the winter-themed release as Arctic Chronicles, with Frost Festival announcements, new themed play and updates across other game modes. Here is a clear guide to the announced release and the beta features players should watch, without confusing test-server previews with guaranteed final patch notes.

When is PUBG Mobile 4.7 releasing?

PUBG Mobile has announced November 12, 2026 for Version 4.7, and its Japanese news site also lists that date for the update. Availability can appear gradually across platforms and regions, so an individual player's download time may not be identical to the announcement date. Check the update prompt in the official app or store rather than downloading a modified installer from an unknown source.

The publisher has described the release as Arctic Chronicles and tied it to the Frost Festival. Ice and snow form the visual theme, but the update involves more than cosmetic scenery. The public beta announcements describe character abilities, a themed vehicle and Classic Mode refinements. Some features may be activated through separate events after the main update arrives.

The Ancient Beast and winter-themed Erangel

The game's November announcements tease an Ancient Beast associated with the winter event. In the third 4.7 beta, parts of Erangel including Pochinki, Rozhok and Mansion were shown with Nordic-inspired changes. That is a beta preview, not a promise that every redesign will arrive exactly as tested. Players should inspect the mode selector and final in-game rules after updating.

The themed beta also describes unusual encounters around the Ancient Beast, including confronting and sealing the creature and exploring areas within it. Treat those descriptions as a sign of the update's direction rather than a verified guide to final loot tables or rewards. Official release notes and the in-game event panel will establish which challenges are active when the version launches.

Aurora Flashstep, Glyph skills and the Pegasus

The second 4.7 beta describes Aurora Flashstep, an active ability involving a double jump and short dash. It also introduces Glyph skills, with passive bonuses distributed randomly at the beginning of themed matches or found at Glyph Altars. The tested effects include faster healing, quicker reloading, supply detection and defensive benefits. The beta states that skill levels range from one to three and that higher levels are obtained randomly rather than upgraded in the middle of a match.

Another preview feature is the Aurora Pegasus, a summonable themed mount that moves on the ground and can take flight. Its appearance and flight behavior change in the Aurora Zone according to the beta notice. A vehicle with unusual vertical movement changes how players approach exposed terrain, but its final speed, availability and competitive balance should be assessed only after the live release.

Classic Mode changes worth checking

The beta notes mention a Canted Foregrip attachment and improvements to the way a vehicle camera behaves while aiming or after a rollover. Earlier testing also describes consumable adjustments, including lighter healing items, faster bandage use and changes to staged weapon reloads. These may affect ordinary firefights even for players who skip themed events. Whether each tested adjustment remains unchanged should be verified against final patch details.

Before playing ranked matches, explore the settings and training area. Compare the handling of a familiar weapon, check available attachments and confirm whether your preferred control layout still feels comfortable. Avoid treating a beta damage description as a final competitive statistic. Patch notes can be refined between test stages and public deployment.

Erangel Subzero is a separate December arrival

The publisher's November 8 preview refers to Erangel Subzero arriving in December, rather than on the main November 12 release date. The test description includes dynamic weather, blizzards, icy terrain and special satellite drops. It also mentions mode-specific equipment and vehicles. These features make a good topic for a later dedicated preview, but the December timing matters when deciding what to expect on launch day.

The beta lists hazards such as temporary blue-zone devices and liquid-nitrogen effects, along with snow transport and a mode-specific weapon pool. Their exact implementation could change. Do not advertise the December mode as immediately playable on November 12 unless it becomes available through a confirmed schedule.

What mobile and emulator players should do now

Mobile players should save their preferred control and sensitivity settings, confirm account access through legitimate login methods and leave suitable space for installation. GameLoop users should check the emulator's own official update process and wait for verified compatibility rather than modifying game files. The publisher has not established identical feature rollout times for every environment in the material reviewed for this article.

Expect additional event announcements around the release, but do not assume that any teased outfit, X-Suit or car finish is awarded automatically by updating. Cosmetic launches can be scheduled separately and may involve event conditions or in-game spending.
