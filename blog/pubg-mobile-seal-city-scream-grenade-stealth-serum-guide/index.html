PUBG Mobile Seal City: Scream Grenade vs Stealth Serum

Published November 8, 2026 · By Digital Racer

PUBG Mobile's third Version 4.7 beta announced two unusual Seal City PvE items: Scream Grenade and Stealth Serum. Both are concerned with enemy attention rather than conventional bullet damage. Players should understand their different tactical purposes and limits before relying on them in a dangerous run.

Scream Grenade attracts enemies

The beta describes a throwable item that draws nearby PvE enemies, causing them to ignore the player briefly. That suggests a decoy or diversion function rather than an ordinary explosive. A good use case may be shifting enemies away from a narrow route, but the exact radius and duration are not established by the preview. An unexpected elite enemy may not react identically to ordinary infected.

Stealth Serum changes enemy attention

Stealth Serum is described as an active-use item after which PvE enemies temporarily ignore the player. That is different from making the player invulnerable or invisible to every possible threat. The beta does not prove that the effect lasts through every interaction or works against all bosses. Keep an exit route and avoid standing in front of an enemy simply because the status appears active.

Combine distractions with route planning

Before using either item, identify the passage you need to reach, the group of enemies blocking it and the safest destination after the effect ends. A decoy thrown without a route may simply move enemies into another teammate's path. Communicate plans so the entire squad understands which direction is safe. Do not use several limited items on a small obstacle if walking around it is easier.

Save items for important encounters

If these items are rare, using one at the first minor obstacle may leave the team with no emergency option during a harder encounter. On the other hand, hoarding every consumable while losing all carried loot is not useful. Set simple criteria: spend a diversion when it prevents a likely loss, saves a valuable extraction or helps the squad regroup. The final in-game descriptions should govern any detailed timing strategy.

Check what survives beta testing

Seal City's enemy roster, supply zones and PvE mechanics were test content. PUBG Mobile explicitly warns that such features can change. Do not rely on a fixed spawn or guaranteed avoidance tactic based on a preview. In the released map, test with inexpensive equipment, document what happens and keep safe fallback routes. Modified clients promising permanent stealth are not legitimate substitutes for normal items.

Example: crossing a blocked street

Imagine a narrow passage to an extraction route with several infected enemies blocking the way. If you have a Scream Grenade, identify where the group should be drawn before throwing it. If you choose Stealth Serum instead, plan a safe destination for when the temporary effect expires. Tell your teammate which method you intend to use and leave room for an unexpected enemy. Neither item should be treated as permanent invisibility. The final game's timing and effects must be checked before relying on a dangerous escape plan.
