PUBG Mobile 4.7 Bentley Collaboration: Vehicle Skin and Card Basics

Published November 15, 2026 · By Digital Racer

PUBG Mobile's 4.7 publicity highlights a Bentley collaboration alongside seasonal rewards. Branded vehicle cosmetics attract attention because the model name and visual finish can carry more meaning than a generic car skin. However, promotional art is not a guarantee that a particular item is free, transferable or already present on an account. This guide covers careful comparison without inventing unannounced prices or guaranteed drop rates.

Identify the actual vehicle finish

Read the active in-game event description and the specific vehicle model name. A branded collaboration may include several colors or finishes, and a poster may show more than one vehicle. Avoid describing every Bentley image as the same item. For an inventory claim, check the visible cosmetic entry and whether the finish is permanent, a trial or an event preview. The difference matters to collectors.

Understand how event acquisition works

Promotions can use direct rewards, challenges, draws or redeemable event items. Do not assume the Bentley cosmetic uses a specific method until the current event explains it. If a draw is offered, read the probability and spending information shown by the game. A low-price first attempt should not be confused with a fixed total cost. Decide on a budget independently of influencer videos.

Car cards are not universal permissions

A car card, gifting token or collaboration ticket is not interchangeable with every other vehicle cosmetic. Its usefulness depends on the live event's supported redemption and gifting rules. A screenshot of a card does not establish that it can be delivered to another account. Check restrictions, supported regions and expiry dates inside the game. Avoid third-party tools promising to convert unrelated tokens into premium cars.

Compare cosmetic value realistically

A rare branded finish may look impressive in a garage or lobby, but it does not automatically improve vehicle speed, handling or protection. Check how it appears during actual gameplay and whether the vehicle model is one you regularly use. The right choice is personal: some collectors value specific brands, while others prefer animated or highly visible designs. Price alone does not prove rarity or usefulness.

Look for evidence rather than slogans

A listing that says full Bentley collection should identify each exact finish, current entitlement and any upgrade stages. Static photos can be copied or outdated. A short video may show a borrowed or temporary item. Do not let phrases such as guaranteed delivery or no risk substitute for the publisher's rules. Account sharing and unapproved virtual item transactions may violate those rules regardless of the screenshot quality.

Check before spending or sharing

Confirm the event window, real price, acquisition path and your payment limits. If the item is unavailable in your region, do not bypass storefront or account security controls. Keep purchase evidence for your own records without sharing login tokens or financial data. A careful decision is based on what the live game confirms, not on the excitement of a collaboration reveal.
