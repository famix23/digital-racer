PUBG Mobile Arctic Chronicles: Safer Pochinki and Rozhok Landings

Published November 15, 2026 · By Digital Racer

The Arctic Chronicles update transforms familiar Erangel areas including Pochinki, Rozhok and Mansion into event locations. A crowded landing can offer quick excitement but little time to collect supplies. Instead of inventing one perfect drop spot, this guide explains how to use flight-path information, line of sight and squad roles to decide whether an Ancient Beast objective is worth contesting.

Read the plane path first

Before marking a hotspot, compare the flight path with the distance to alternate compounds and vehicle spawns you actually recognize. A named event landmark near the flight line will often attract more attention than a distant location, although lobby behavior varies. If the squad wants early combat, choose a safe landing within sight of escape cover. If it wants placement, begin outside the busiest cluster.

Keep the squad close enough to trade

Split landings can work for organized teams, but scattering across several distant rooftops leaves a knocked player impossible to help. Assign two players to the main loot cluster, one to watch a common approach and one to identify a retreat route. Communicate weapon availability rather than guessing who can fight. An unarmed teammate should not sprint toward an Ancient Beast interaction just because the icon appears important.

Approach sealing objectives carefully

The official update describes shooting down sealing towers, activating pedestals and entering the beast after the seal is restored. Each interaction creates a pause during which you may be vulnerable to opponents outside the objective space. Clear nearby angles, listen for movement and have another teammate hold cover before committing. A squad should understand the objective sequence but remain ready to abandon it when the fight turns unfavorable.

Treat special spaces differently

The release describes a shared space inside the beast where combat is disabled. That does not mean the entire route into it is safe. Check the actual boundary before relaxing, and never assume a no-combat rule extends to nearby streets, roofs or exits. If a Hero's Medal enables a special reward, verify its status in the match interface rather than planning around a teammate's unconfirmed memory of the beta.

Rotate away before the zone traps you

A themed attraction can distract a squad from the first safe-zone timer. As soon as the event interaction is finished, check the circle, vehicle availability and nearby gunfire. Use cover between compounds instead of crossing a main road just because an event marker points that way. If enemies hold the obvious exit, a longer route may preserve armor and healing supplies for the next fight.

Practice two landing plans

Prepare a contested plan for when the squad has immediate weapons and a fallback plan for weak opening loot. Run each in several normal matches and review where players were separated or caught reloading. Aim to learn why a route works, not memorize a map from one viral clip. Patch changes and enemy behavior can make an older tactic unreliable, so keep adapting.
