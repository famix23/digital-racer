PUBG Mobile 4.7 New X-Suit: Three Forms and Hidden Zenith Form

Published November 15, 2026 · By Digital Racer

PUBG Mobile's 4.7 cosmetic announcement highlights an X-Suit with three distinct forms and a hidden Zenith form that becomes available after fully upgrading all three. That headline can be exciting, but cosmetic progression is easy to misunderstand when a trailer shows completed effects. This guide explains the difference between discovering a set, owning its base form and meeting an upgrade requirement.

Three forms do not mean three free suits

A trailer may display multiple visual variations in one sequence. It does not establish that all three are free, delivered at purchase or available in every region. Open the current in-game X-Suit event and read how each form is obtained and upgraded. The name of a form is not enough to confirm its entitlement. Look for an ownership marker on the actual inventory or upgrade screen.

What hidden Zenith means

The official Version 4.7 press information states that fully upgrading all three forms unlocks the hidden Zenith form. It does not follow that an account with only one upgraded variant owns Zenith. Confirm the exact completion condition shown for each of the three forms and check whether additional materials are required. Treat social clips showing an unlocked final animation as demonstrations, not evidence of the cost or availability on your account.

Distinguish cosmetic stages

Upgradeable outfits can alter appearance, animation or effects at specific stages. Do not assume a base unlock includes every presentation feature visible in promotional footage. When comparing collections, write the current form, stage and confirmed effects separately. A headline such as X-Suit owned is less informative than a clear inventory view of the specific form and its current progression.

Compare value with your play habits

If you frequently use a particular outfit, the visual design may matter more than a limited-time banner. Decide which form you actually want and whether you would still enjoy it without the hidden version. An expensive upgrade chain is optional entertainment, not a competitive requirement. Cosmetic prestige should never pressure a player to spend beyond a predetermined limit or transfer login details to a third party.

Avoid misleading ownership claims

Videos can be recorded on beta, preview or other players' accounts. A claimed maxed outfit may use a static image, temporary trial or staged demonstration. Genuine ownership must be assessed through the live item interface and subject to publisher rules. Do not use tools advertising instant X-Suit unlocks; client edits do not create legitimate entitlements and can put both devices and game accounts at risk.

Keep a transparent upgrade checklist

Record each form, the current stage, the material requirement displayed and any event end date. Stop after reaching the level you intended rather than chasing a completion bonus impulsively. If a patch changes the event, update the checklist. Digital Racer coverage should differentiate confirmed announcements from individual purchases, allowing readers to compare cosmetics without implying that anyone has received items they have not earned.
