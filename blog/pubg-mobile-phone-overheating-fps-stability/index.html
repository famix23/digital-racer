PUBG Mobile Phone Overheating: Protect FPS and Battery Health

Published November 18, 2026 · By Digital Racer

Find practical causes of thermal FPS drops without unsafe cooling tricks. This Digital Racer guide focuses on decisions a player can check in ordinary gameplay or through official settings, not secret unlocks or promises of certain results. Exact conditions depend on mode, device and current update, so begin with the rules and controls the game actually exposes. Aim for a repeatable process that improves awareness, safety and consistency instead of copying a single dramatic clip.

Distinguish a thermal trend

If frame rate begins smoothly and decays as the phone warms, heat may be involved. Compare several short sessions under similar conditions. A drop that happens immediately on every launch may instead be a game cap or unsupported setting.

Lower the sustained workload

Use supported graphics settings that the device can maintain over a full match. A short period at the highest preset is less useful than consistent responsive input. Avoid forcing hidden options through modified game files.

Remove unnecessary background load

Close demanding applications and downloads before a session. A screen recorder, navigation overlay or background synchronization may consume resources and create additional heat. Do not disable critical protection or permission checks to gain a trivial benchmark increase.

Improve the physical environment

Avoid direct sunlight and insulating the phone with heavy coverings. Use a safe surface and allow heat to dissipate. Condensation-prone cooling methods can damage electronics; extreme temperature changes are not a sensible performance strategy.

Monitor charging habits

Charging during an intensive match can add heat. Follow the phone manufacturer's guidance and avoid damaged cables or questionable accessories. If the device becomes unusually hot, stop the session and inspect its condition rather than continuing to chase FPS.

Compare sustained smoothness

Judge frame-time consistency and touch response over a similar session length. A slightly lower preset that stays stable may improve competitive experience. Revisit settings after major updates because a device's workload can change with new content.

Apply this method and compare results

For a reliable comparison, note the present settings and record the symptom before changing anything. Make only one supported adjustment, restart the affected application when appropriate and retest the same situation. Revert changes that worsen stability, security or comfort. A percentage or benchmark without practical improvement is not a good reason to keep a tweak. Use only official game and operating-system controls where possible.
