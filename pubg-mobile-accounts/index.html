How to compare PUBG Mobile accounts

Different players value different features. Compare the account level and collection level first, then check whether individual upgradeable weapons are at the level you want. A listing mentioning M416 Glacier, AKM, X-Suits, mythic fashion or a vehicle skin does not necessarily mean those items are maxed. Open the product detail page to review the stated item levels, images and price.

Confirm the details before enquiring

Check the game region, server, current rank, linked sign-in methods, access arrangements and any recovery restrictions. Ask for current screenshots or other evidence for costly collections, because inventories and availability can change. Pricing is displayed in USD; the final payable amount and supported payment and delivery method should be confirmed before any purchase.

Understand account-transfer risks

PUBG Mobile account transfers may conflict with the game's terms or lead to restrictions. Transferred accounts can also present recovery or ownership disputes. Read the account safety guide, do not share passwords with strangers, and do not assume an account can be permanently secured merely because a listing mentions full access.
