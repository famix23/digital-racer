PUBG Mobile UC Top-Up Safety Guide

UC is PUBG Mobile's in-game currency. A discounted top-up can appear simple, but the important distinction is whether the transaction uses a publisher-authorized purchasing channel. A seller's claim of speed or safety is not proof.

Publisher warning: PUBG Mobile recommends authorized purchasing services and states that unauthorized purchases can lead to deduction of UC and account penalties, including bans. Read official guidance before paying a third party.

Where to begin when purchasing UC

Start with the purchase flow inside the official game and any authorized channels identified by the game publisher for your region. Availability, currency, payment methods and event bonuses can differ between locations and over time. Check that you are using the genuine publisher or authorized provider, rather than a similarly named website or a link supplied in a private chat.

Do not assume that a service is approved because it advertises a player-ID-only top-up or has positive-looking screenshots. PUBG Mobile's support article about unauthorized services says unauthorized transactions can violate its rules. An apparent balance increase at first is not a guarantee that the UC will remain available.

What a UID does — and does not prove

A player identifier (UID) helps distinguish one game profile from another. It does not verify the identity of the person offering a top-up, the authorization of their payment method, or their ability to deliver. Check the recipient identifier carefully whenever using an authorized purchase flow. A single wrong digit can refer to a different account and may be difficult to correct.

A normal product enquiry should never require you to reveal a game password, email password, bank OTP, authentication app code or social-login recovery code. If someone asks for these, stop. Never grant remote control of your phone or computer for a routine UC purchase.

How to assess suspicious offers

  • Unusually steep discounts: compare the total amount payable with the authorized in-game or regional purchase price, including fees.
  • Pressure and secrecy: do not send payment because a seller claims a special price expires in minutes.
  • Missing transaction details: a screenshot of UC or a chat message is not equivalent to a verifiable order from an authorized provider.
  • Payment-method surprises: changes in recipient, region or payment method after agreeing on a price can indicate added risk.
  • Account-access requests: a top-up enquiry should never become a demand for your login session, password or OTP.

If a purchase is delayed or missing

  1. Keep your order number, authorized-provider receipt, amount, currency, date and the UID entered. Avoid sharing full bank-card details.
  2. Check whether the authorized provider reports the purchase as pending, failed or completed before making a second payment.
  3. Consult the game's current support instructions for delayed delivery and use official player support when appropriate.
  4. If an unauthorized payment was taken without delivery, preserve the evidence and contact your payment provider about available dispute options. Avoid posting private information publicly.

Refunds and restrictions

Digital goods are often subject to different refund and chargeback rules from physical products. Publishers may reverse invalid purchases or take other account action. Do not assume that an intermediary's refund promise supersedes the game publisher's rules. Read the transaction terms before paying, and keep the proof of any purchase made through an authorized channel.
