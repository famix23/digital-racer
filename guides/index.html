Why these guides exist

Shopping pages show a particular offer at a particular moment. A guide should help you understand the underlying terminology and risks independently of any offer. For example, a claimed number of upgradeable weapon skins is not the same as a count of max-level weapons. Similarly, a screenshot of an X-Suit does not prove that the listed account can be transferred or that its original owner cannot recover it.

We therefore separate the catalog from learning material. Guide articles do not promise that any third-party transaction is safe or allowed. If you need help with a lost account, a penalty or an abnormal purchase, the publisher's official support service is the appropriate place to start.

Editorial approach

These guides distinguish general game terminology from actual transaction permissions. Claims about account transfers or unofficial UC purchases are linked to publisher documentation. Game events and cosmetics can change, and not every historical item is available in every regional version; consult the current in-game event rules for specific availability.

We avoid claiming firsthand tests, game-publisher endorsement, guaranteed delivery or a risk-free account transfer. If a page elsewhere on the store appears to contradict a publisher rule, follow the publisher's current terms. You can contact Digital Racer about an inaccurate listing.
