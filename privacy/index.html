Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026.

This page explains the information involved when you browse digitalracer.store and choose to contact Digital Racer. The website displays a catalog; orders are discussed outside the website using WhatsApp.

Information involved in using the site

When you visit the site, its hosting and security infrastructure may process technical information such as your IP address, request details, browser information and timestamps for delivery, performance and protection of the service. If you choose to contact us using WhatsApp, your contact details, messages and any game identifier you voluntarily share in that conversation may be used to respond to your enquiry, discuss delivery and assist with an order.

Please avoid sending account passwords, one-time verification codes or unrelated sensitive information in enquiries.

External services

The website links to WhatsApp, a third-party service provided by Meta. Opening the link and communicating there are subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices. Our website is served through a web hosting and security provider, which may process technical usage information.

Advertising and cookies

If advertising is enabled on this website, third-party advertising vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies helps Google and its partners serve relevant advertisements. You can manage personalized Google advertising at My Ad Center and learn about some industry opt-out choices at aboutads.info. See Google's advertising technologies information for more details.

Where required for visitors in the EEA, United Kingdom or Switzerland, advertising consent options must be presented through an appropriate consent-management platform before relevant advertising technologies are used. The appearance of this policy does not mean that Google advertising is already active.

Your questions and choices

You can decline to open external order links and adjust cookie controls in your browser. For questions about information you directly provided to Digital Racer, or to ask about correction or deletion, reach the store through the contact page. Requests are handled in accordance with applicable obligations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this notice when the website's tools, advertising or ordering practices change. The revision date at the top indicates the most recent published version.
